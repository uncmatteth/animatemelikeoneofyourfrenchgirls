# Compare the pictures and the motion

Grade each applicable item **yes**, **partial** or **no**, with the viewed frame or
time. A technical receipt is not a visual verdict. Unknown stays unknown.

1. Each character matches the current identity references, including hair and hands.
2. The set's fixed anchors match across every shot/reverse shot.
3. The action has a visible setup, contact, result and reaction.
4. Exactly the intended speaker moves their mouth during each dialogue cue.
5. Feet plant; hands meet objects; ropes have coherent endpoints and tension.
6. Every limb remains anatomically readable throughout transitions.
7. No duplicate outline, stray person, unexplained prop or changing symbol appears.
8. Door, mechanism, water and lighting states change only at their authored events.
9. Movement includes meaningful subject action; camera-only movement does not pass.
10. The image retains the reference's style, detail, framing and color relationships.
11. Dialogue and effects align, speech remains intelligible and audio is not clipped.
12. The actual final encode has been inspected, with any limitations reported.

Review the first pose, anticipation, contact, maximal load and settled pose. Include
frames inside fast transitions and compare adjacent shots. Determinism: render A,
then B, then A again; the two A frames must match. Spatial checks should compare
named world-space anchors, not only an attractive camera crop.
